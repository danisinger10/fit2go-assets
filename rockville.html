Frequently Asked Questions

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes. We supplement your building's equipment with kettlebells, suspension trainers, mini bands, and recovery tools so every session feels like a premium studio experience. We also coordinate with concierge teams to keep the space reserved for you.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square or Choice Hotels HQ?

Absolutely. We routinely support teams at Choice Hotels, Westat, Montgomery College, and nearby law firms. We'll sync with building security, bring any specialty equipment, and keep workouts efficient so you can get back to meetings on time.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes—weather permitting, we map strength and conditioning circuits that start at your door and move onto the trails. Think interval runs along Rock Creek, hill sprints by Lake Needwood, and cooldown mobility work back at home.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

Once you complete the consultation call, we typically schedule your assessment within 3-5 days. We reserve sunrise slots (5:30-7:30 AM) for clients who need to beat traffic, and we can adjust session cadence around hybrid office schedules.

What does the first 4-week phase include and how does pricing work?

Your first phase covers the assessment, personalized programming, two to three weekly training sessions, nutrition coaching, and in-app progress tracking. We bill in 4-week blocks so you always know your cadence and can adjust frequency before the next phase renews.
