FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes. We supply kettlebells, adjustable dumbbells, benches, sliders, and mobility tools to complement your amenity gym. Our coaches arrive early to stage the room and sanitize equipment before and after every session.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square?

Absolutely. We already work with teams at Choice Hotels HQ, Westat, and Montgomery College. We coordinate building access, bring supplemental gear, and can schedule recurring lunch-hour sessions.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes—when the weather cooperates we stage circuits right off the trails, using bands, sleds, and bodyweight conditioning. We always prepare an indoor backup plan so weather never derails your momentum.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

Consultations happen within 48 hours, assessments within 3–5 days, and training can begin the same week. Our Rockville coaches open their schedules as early as 5:30 a.m. so you can train before hitting 270 or the Metro.

What does the first 4-week phase include?

Expect two to three coached sessions weekly, personalized homework workouts, and app-based accountability. Week four includes a reassessment so we can recommend your next phase—whether that’s continuing weekly, adding hybrid sessions, or inviting a training partner.
