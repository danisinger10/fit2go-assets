Rockville FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Absolutely. We supplement on-site equipment with kettlebells, power bands, sleds, and recovery tools so each session feels like a concierge studio. We sanitize everything before and after use and adapt to the space available.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square or Research Boulevard?

Yes. Many clients meet us at corporate gyms for early-morning or lunch sessions. We coordinate building access, bring specialty tools that your gym may not have, and keep workouts under 45 minutes so you're back at your desk on time.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Outdoor conditioning is one of our specialties. We plan interval runs, sled pushes, and strength circuits using park benches, hills, and portable equipment while tracking heart-rate and pace via the Fit2Go app.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

After your consultation call, we typically complete the assessment within 3–4 days and launch training within a week. Sunrise sessions (5:30–7:00 a.m.) are reserved specifically for commuters who want to beat traffic.

What does the first 4-week phase include?

Your initial phase includes 8–12 training sessions (based on chosen cadence), mobility homework, macro guidance, and weekly accountability touchpoints. We finish with a progress audit to map the next four weeks.

How does billing work for Rockville clients?

We operate on rolling 4-week phases with secure autopay. You can adjust cadence (2–4 sessions per week) or pause with 7-day notice. All sessions include travel, equipment, and app access—no hidden facility fees.
