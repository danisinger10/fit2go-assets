Rockville FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes. We supply adjustable dumbbells, kettlebells, TRX, resistance bands, sleds, and recovery tools. If your building gym has limited space, we design stations that fit neatly beside existing equipment so neighbors can still access the room. We'll also recommend any small items worth keeping on hand between sessions.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square?

Absolutely. Many of our clients work at Westat, Choice Hotels HQ, and Montgomery County agencies near Rockville Town Square. We coordinate building access with your facilities team, bring any specialty gear they don't provide, and keep sessions aligned with your meeting schedule.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes. We love pairing strength work at home with conditioning along the Rock Creek trails, Mattie J. T. Stepanek Park, or Lake Needwood paths. Your trainer will map low-traffic intervals, bring portable equipment, and plan around daylight and weather.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

Once you book your consultation, we typically complete the assessment within 5-7 days. Early-morning sessions starting at 5:30 a.m. are popular, so we'll lock in your spot during the vetting call with Ari to make sure it fits both calendars.

What does the first 4-week phase include?

Phase one blends three coached sessions per week, individualized mobility and conditioning homework, a customized nutrition roadmap, and weekly progress reviews with Ari. We reassess key movement patterns at the end of week four and adjust programming or frequency before moving into your next block.
