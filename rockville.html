Rockville FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes. We outfit your space with adjustable dumbbells, kettlebells, TRX, mini-sled options, and mobility tools that complement whatever your building already provides. Sessions are designed to set up and break down in under five minutes so neighbors aren’t disrupted.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square or Pike & Rose?

Absolutely. We routinely coach teams at employers like Westat, Choice Hotels HQ, and Montgomery College. We coordinate building access, bring any missing specialty equipment, and tailor 30–45 minute sessions to your meeting schedule.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes. We map interval runs, hiking strength circuits, and recovery walks along Rock Creek Trail, Lake Needwood, and West End Park fields. Your trainer brings portable equipment and adapts to weather conditions with indoor alternatives when needed.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

Once you submit the form, Ari will schedule your vetting call within 24 hours. Early-morning slots (5:30–7:30 a.m.) are popular, so we’ll confirm availability during that call and prioritize your preferred days before your assessment is booked.

What does the first 4-week phase include and how does pricing work?

Phase one pairs 2–3 coached sessions per week with nutrition coaching, habit assignments, and in-app accountability. Pricing is reviewed on the vetting call based on session frequency and location, then billed in 4-week blocks so you always know what’s next—no hidden fees or long-term contracts.

Do you also serve nearby neighborhoods outside Rockville?

Yes. We serve Rockville and nearby areas by appointment only—no walk-ins. If you live along the 355 corridor or close to North Bethesda, let us know your cross streets on the intake form so we can confirm travel windows for your trainer.
