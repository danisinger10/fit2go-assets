Rockville FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes—our trainers supplement whatever equipment your building provides. We bring adjustable dumbbells, kettlebells, sleds, mobility tools, and recovery gear so your King Farm or Fallsgrove amenities feel like a private studio. We'll also coordinate with building staff if space reservations are needed.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square?

Absolutely. We regularly serve teams at Westat, Choice Hotels, and Montgomery County offices. We'll coordinate building access, align with your security protocols, and bring any specialty equipment to complement your onsite setup.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes. We design outdoor strength and conditioning blocks along Rock Creek Trailheads, Lake Needwood overlooks, and Woodley Gardens Park. We handle permits when required and bring portable gear like battle ropes, sandbags, and sleds.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

After your consultation and assessment, most clients begin within 5-7 days. We hold blocks as early as 5:00 a.m. so you can train before Rockville Pike gets busy or before heading to the Metro or MARC.

What does the first 4-week phase include?

Your opening phase includes 2-3 weekly sessions, a mobility and strength plan inside the Fit2Go app, nutrition coaching with macro targets, and weekly check-ins with Ari. We'll review progress at the end of week four and recommend the best cadence moving forward—no surprises.
