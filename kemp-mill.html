Shabbat-Friendly Scheduling

Plan your training week around candle lighting, minyan, and chagim

We never book sessions on Shabbat or chagim. Instead, we plan your week with pre-Shabbat strength or mobility and a light post-Yom Tov reset. If you observe carrying limits inside the eruv, we’ll design an at-home plan that fits—no compromises on results.

Early mornings before school drop-off at YISE, mid-day breaks after errands at Shalom Kosher, or post-sunset sessions once the kids are settled—we map everything to your family rhythm. Expect calendars synced to zmanim and automated reminders so you’re always prepared.
