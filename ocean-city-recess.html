How it works

Mondays: Free all-levels class for anyone in Ocean City.

Wednesdays & Fridays: $5–$10 drop-ins if you want an extra push.

Location: Bayside Park & neighborhood green spaces. We keep it walkable and kid-friendly.

What to expect: 45-minute sessions, loaner mats and bands, strength + cardio mix, led by Fit2Go coaches.

Weekly rhythm that fits your budget

Monday · Free Community Recess

Show up, move together, and feel good without spending a dollar.

  • All fitness levels welcome—bring friends and family.
  • Simple equipment provided; sneakers are all you need.
  • Weather-friendly plan with covered options nearby.

Wednesday · Momentum Session

$5 drop-in (or pay what you can). Small-group coaching to keep you progressing.

  • Strength + cardio intervals in 45 minutes.
  • Form cues and modifications for every body.
  • Stay accountable between free Mondays.

Friday · Feel-Good Finish

$10 drop-in. A fun end-of-week reset with community vibes.

  • Low-impact options and core stability focus.
  • Stretch + breathing cooldown to start the weekend right.
  • Kids can tag along—space for strollers and wheels.

Quick answers

Do I need to register?

Reserve a spot below so we bring enough equipment, but walk-ups are welcome for Monday classes.

What if it rains?

We move to a nearby covered space or shift to a light movement + stretching session. We’ll text updates two hours prior.

Are kids allowed?

Yes. We plan stations that leave room for strollers and give parents space to move safely.

Who leads the sessions?

Fit2Go certified trainers with CPR/First Aid and experience coaching mixed-level groups.
