Rockville FAQs

Do you bring equipment to Rockville apartment gyms like those in King Farm or Fallsgrove?

Yes. We supplement each building's setup with kettlebells, adjustable dumbbells, TRX systems, sleds, battle ropes, and mobility tools. Your trainer coordinates with building management if access badges or loading docks are required so workouts start right on time.

Can you train me at my office gym near Rockville Town Square?

Absolutely. We support employers such as Westat, Choice Hotels HQ, ZeniMax, and Montgomery College. Our team navigates building security, schedules around meeting blocks, and provides corporate wellness reporting so leadership sees the ROI.

Do you run outdoor sessions on the Rock Creek trails or near Lake Needwood?

Yes—we design seasonal programming that uses Rock Creek Regional Park, Lake Needwood loops, and Woodley Gardens parks for conditioning, rucking, and recovery. We monitor weather, bring portable mats, and adjust intensity based on trail conditions.

How soon can I start if I need early mornings before Rockville Pike traffic?

Consultations are available within 24 hours. Most Rockville clients begin training within 7 days after the assessment. We regularly book 5:30–7:00 a.m. slots to beat I-270 congestion or align with MARC/Metro schedules.

What does the first 4-week phase include?

After your comprehensive assessment, you receive a custom training plan (2-4 sessions per week), nutrition coaching, and access to our app with habit tracking. Pricing is reviewed during your vetting call so it aligns with your budget before we meet in person—no surprises.
