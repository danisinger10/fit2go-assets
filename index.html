The Ghost in Your Genes

For decades, we believed we were blank slates. Modern biology says otherwise. You carry more than eye color—you inherit your grandmother’s survival strategies.

Transgenerational epigenetic inheritance shows that trauma and extreme stress can flip chemical switches on DNA and pass them forward. When you feel an uncontrollable urge, you might be executing an outdated survival protocol.

Example: The Dutch Hunger Winter study found that grandchildren of famine survivors were more prone to obesity and metabolic disorders—even though they never experienced scarcity themselves.

The Warning Light, Not the Driver

Impulses are information, not identity. When your body says, “Eat everything now,” it might be repeating an ancestral script that once ensured survival.

The reframe: “My body is running a protocol” gives you the space to choose a new response.
