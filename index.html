Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer sessions on Shabbat or Jewish holidays?

We do not schedule sessions during Shabbat or Yom Tov. Trainers are available Motzei Shabbat and throughout the week to respect your observance.

Can you train multiple family members at once?

Yes! We design sessions for couples, families, and chavura groups. Your coach will tailor the workout to each participant’s ability level.

Do you provide equipment?

Your trainer brings portable equipment like resistance bands, kettlebells, and suspension straps. We also help you set up a home gym if desired.

Is virtual coaching available?

Absolutely. We offer Zoom-based workouts with the same accountability and feedback as in-person sessions—ideal for travel or snow days.
