Questions About Getting Started?

Our team is ready to guide you through each step—from the first consult to your long-term strategy.

What happens during the first strategy session?

You’ll meet 1:1 with a Fit2Go Rockville coach for a full lifestyle audit, movement screen, and goal-setting session. We’ll map out the right coaching track and give you a 30-day action plan before you leave.

Do you accommodate previous injuries or mobility limitations?

Yes. Every program starts with a mobility assessment. Your coach coordinates with your PT or medical provider, and we adjust sessions in real time to protect your joints while rebuilding strength.

Can I train with a partner or small group?

We offer semi-private options for partners or colleagues who share similar goals. You’ll still receive individualized programming while benefiting from shared accountability.

What makes Fit2Go different from other Rockville studios?

Our concierge approach combines private studio access, elite coaching, and data-backed accountability. We handle the details—from scheduling to nutrition—so you can focus on showing up and executing.
