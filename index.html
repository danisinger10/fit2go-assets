Frequently Asked Questions

Do you train on Fridays or during the Yamim Tovim?

We offer Friday sessions until two hours before candle lighting and pause training on Shabbat and Yom Tov. Need a pre-holiday workout? We'll schedule earlier in the week and provide at-home guides.

Can my spouse or friend join the sessions?

Absolutely. We offer partner and small-group options so you can train with your spouse, chavrusa, or neighbor while sharing the investment.

Do you bring equipment?

Yes. We supply kettlebells, resistance bands, mats, and mobility tools. We can also design routines around the equipment you already own.

How soon can I start?

Submit the form above or call us directly. Most new Kemp Mill clients start within 7 days after their wellness assessment.
