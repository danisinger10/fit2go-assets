Questions Before You Apply?

Do I need equipment?

No. Fit2Go trainers bring the core equipment needed for effective private sessions, including items like resistance, strength, mobility, and conditioning tools.

How much space do I need?

Most sessions can work in a living room, garage, patio, building gym, office gym, or outdoor area. Your trainer adapts the plan to the space available.

Do you help with nutrition?

Yes. Nutrition coaching can include practical meal guidance, habit support, calorie and macro targets when appropriate, and ongoing accountability.

Can I train with someone else?

Yes. Semi-private training is a strong fit for couples, friends, family members, or small groups who want accountability together.

What happens after I apply?

Your information is reviewed and you are routed to schedule a Fit2Go consultation. The team confirms your goals, location, and best-fit training format.

Is this only for advanced clients?

No. The program is customized for beginners, returning exercisers, busy adults, and experienced clients who want a more convenient coaching system.
