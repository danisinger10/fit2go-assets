Frequently Asked Questions

Do you train during Shabbat or chagim?

No. We close before candle-lighting on Fridays and resume Motzei Shabbos or Sunday morning, ensuring full respect for halachic observance.

What equipment do I need?

We bring everything required—from resistance bands to adjustable weights. If you already own equipment, we’ll incorporate it safely.

Can you coordinate with my doctor or physical therapist?

Absolutely. We work alongside local healthcare providers to keep your training medically aligned, especially post-PT or post-partum.

Do you offer programs for teens?

Yes! From Bnei Akiva athletes to students at Yeshiva of Greater Washington, we design age-appropriate training with parental involvement.
