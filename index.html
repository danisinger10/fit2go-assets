Rockville Personal Training FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking your consultation.

How does Fit2Go's in-home personal training work?

Your coach arrives with commercial-grade equipment, sets up in the space you have, and guides every rep. You get the entire session to yourself—no commuting, no waiting for machines, no distractions.

Do you offer nutrition and accountability support?

Yes. Each Rockville membership includes weekly nutrition check-ins, habit coaching, and access to our Fit2Go app so you can log meals from Founding Farmers, Matchbox, or wherever you dine in the Pike District.

What if I'm managing an injury?

Your coach integrates corrective exercise, mobility work, and communication with your medical team. We routinely support clients working with Adventist HealthCare, Shady Grove orthopedists, and local PT clinics.
