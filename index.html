FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work around Shabbat, Yom Tov, and fast days?

Absolutely. Our trainers provide pre-holiday programming, lighter recovery sessions during fast weeks, and complete rest planning for Shabbat so you can recharge without losing momentum.

What equipment do I need for in-home sessions?

None. Your trainer arrives with everything required, from weights and resistance bands to mats and recovery tools. We can also design workouts using equipment you already own.

Is nutrition guidance kosher-friendly?

Yes. Our nutrition support respects kosher guidelines and offers tips for communal meals, simchas, and Shabbos menus to keep you on track.

How soon can I start?

Consultations are usually available within a week. After your assessment, most clients begin their personalized program within 5-7 days.
