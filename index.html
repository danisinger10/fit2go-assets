Frequently Asked Questions

Do you bring equipment?

Yes! We bring everything from dumbbells and resistance bands to balance tools. All equipment is sanitized before arriving at your home.

Can sessions be in Hebrew?

Absolutely. Many of our trainers speak Hebrew and are comfortable integrating Hebrew instructions, tefillah-inspired mindfulness, and Jewish music playlists.

What about modesty requirements?

We pair you with trainers who respect tzniut guidelines. Female clients may request female-only trainers, and sessions are tailored to your comfort level.

How soon can we start?

After your complimentary consultation, we match you with a trainer within 48 hours. Most Kemp Mill clients start their first session within a week.
