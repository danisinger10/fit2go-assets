Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only serve Rockville?

Rockville is our home base, and we also serve surrounding Montgomery County neighborhoods including Bethesda, Potomac, and North Bethesda. Virtual coaching is available nationwide.

What if I don’t have any equipment?

No problem! Your trainer brings everything you need for in-home sessions and can recommend affordable gear to keep at home. We also design bodyweight-friendly routines.

Can you work around injuries or medical considerations?

Yes. Our trainers are certified and experienced in post-physical-therapy programming. We’ll coordinate with your healthcare providers to ensure every session is safe and effective.

How fast will I see results?

Most Fit2Go clients feel stronger and more energized within the first month. Visible changes in body composition typically appear within 8–12 weeks with consistent training and nutrition guidance.
